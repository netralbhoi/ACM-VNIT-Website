Areas of focus.

Six technical domains. Each with its own community, projects, workshops, and rhythm. Find yours, or explore them all.

Hover a domain to explore

Domain details

01

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, neural networks, computer vision, and natural language processing. We explore AI fundamentals through hands-on projects and research reading groups.

Deep Learning Computer Vision NLP Research

02

Web Development

Full-stack engineering — from front-end interfaces to back-end systems and cloud infrastructure. Members build real products used by the VNIT community.

React Node.js Databases DevOps

03

Competitive Programming

Algorithmic thinking and problem solving at the highest level. Regular contests, editorial discussions, and coaching for ICPC and other prestigious competitions.

Algorithms Data Structures ICPC Codeforces

04

Cybersecurity

Ethical hacking, CTF competitions, network security, and cryptography. We believe understanding offense is essential to building great defenses.

CTF Cryptography Network Security Ethical Hacking

05

Open Source

Contributing to the global open-source ecosystem. We guide members through their first contributions and organize events around open-source culture and collaboration.

GitHub GSoC Contributions Community

06

Internet of Things

Embedded systems, sensor networks, and the intersection of hardware and software. From Arduino prototypes to smart campus projects.

Arduino Raspberry Pi Embedded Sensors
