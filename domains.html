Getting involved

HOW DOMAIN MEMBERSHIP WORKS.

Step 01

JOIN ACM-VNIT

Become a member of the chapter first. Membership is open to all VNIT students — any branch, any year. No prerequisites required.

Step 02

PICK YOUR DOMAIN

Choose one or more domains that match your interests. Attend introductory sessions and meet the domain leads who'll guide your onboarding.

Step 03

START BUILDING

Dive into workshops, join an active project, or compete in your domain's next event. The community will meet you where you are.
