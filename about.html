COMPUTING
IS A CRAFT.
WE TAKE IT
SERIOUSLY.

ACM-VNIT is the official student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, one of India's premier computing communities.

We've been pushing boundaries and challenging conventions since 2009. From research and development to open-source contributions and large-scale technical events, we turn ideas into real-world impact, with 5,000+ students reached and 15+ years of continuous activity.

Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology

"We believe computing is one of the most powerful tools available to humanity. Our goal is to make sure VNIT students are equipped, both technically and professionally, to use it well."

What we do

THREE WAYS WE OPERATE.

01

LEARN

Workshops, reading groups, bootcamps, and one-on-one mentorship across six technical domains. Learning here is hands-on, peer-driven, and connected to real practice.

Workshops Mentorship Bootcamps

02

BUILD

We ship real software used by the VNIT community. From web platforms to competitive programming tools, our projects have impact beyond demos.

Real Products Open Source In Production

03

COMPETE

From ICPC regionals to national hackathons, our members compete at the highest levels. We prepare together and celebrate each other's wins.

ICPC Hackathons CTFs
