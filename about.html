Our story

SERIOUS ABOUT COMPUTING.

ACM-VNIT is the official student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur — one of India's most active computing communities.

We've been pushing boundaries and challenging conventions since 2009. From research and development to open-source contributions and large-scale technical events, we turn ideas into real-world impact.

With 15+ years of uninterrupted activity and 5,000+ students impacted through events, mentorship, and hands-on training, our members go on to work at leading technology companies, pursue research at top institutions, and start their own ventures.

We believe computing is one of the most powerful tools available to humanity. Our goal is to make sure VNIT students are equipped — technically and professionally — to use it well.
