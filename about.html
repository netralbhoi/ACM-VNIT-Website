01 / About

Who we are.

A community of builders, thinkers, and problem-solvers.

ACM-VNIT is the student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur. We are one of India's most active computing student communities.

Since our founding in 2016, we've built a culture where serious technical work meets genuine community. Our members go on to work at leading technology companies, pursue research at top institutions, and start their own ventures.

We believe computing is one of the most powerful tools available to humanity. Our goal is to make sure VNIT students are equipped to use it well.

What we do

01

Learn

Workshops, reading groups, bootcamps, and one-on-one mentorship across six technical domains. Learning here is hands-on, peer-driven, and connected to real practice.

02

Build

We ship real software used by the VNIT community. From web platforms to competitive programming tools, our projects have impact beyond demos.

03

Compete

From ICPC regionals to national hackathons, our members compete at the highest levels. We prepare together and celebrate each other's wins.

Our vision

"To be the most impactful computing community in Indian technical education."
