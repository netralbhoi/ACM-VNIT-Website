ACM-VNIT is the official student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur. Since 2009, we’ve been fostering a culture of technical rigor, open-source innovation, and engineering leadership.

01

Research & Open Source

Publishing research, contributing to global OSS repositories, and mentoring students for competitive programs like GSoC.

02

Engineering & Systems

Hands-on masterclasses covering full-stack architecture, machine learning models, cloud DevOps, and competitive programming.

03

Community Leadership

Connecting 5,000+ student engineers with alumni networks, industry leaders, and national-level hackathons.
