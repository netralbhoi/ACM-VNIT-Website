01 / About

Computing
is a craft.
We take it
seriously.

ACM-VNIT is the student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery at VNIT Nagpur — one of India's premier technical institutes. We are a community of students serious about computer science, engineering, and the future of technology.

Founded in 2016, we have grown to 500+ members across all engineering branches, united by curiosity and a commitment to pushing the boundaries of what we can build together.
