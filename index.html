COMPUTING
IS A CRAFT.

ACM-VNIT is the official student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery at VNIT Nagpur — one of India's premier engineering institutions. We've been pushing boundaries and challenging conventions since 2009.

From research and open-source contributions to large-scale technical events, we turn ideas into real-world impact — with 5,000+ students reached and 15+ years of uninterrupted activity.
