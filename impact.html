Timeline

WHERE WE'VE BEEN.

Six milestones across fifteen years that define who we are today.

2009

FOUNDED

Origin

ACM-VNIT established as the official student chapter at VNIT Nagpur, starting with a group of students committed to building a serious computing culture on campus.

2015

FIRST FLAGSHIP HACKATHON

Event

Organized VNIT's first student-led 24-hour hackathon, which would lay the groundwork for our annual flagship hackathon (Insomnia), proving that students could run serious technical events.

2018

ICPC QUALIFIERS

Competition

ACM-VNIT teams qualified for ICPC Asia Regionals for the first time — a major milestone that put VNIT on the competitive programming map.

2021

DEPLOYED CAMPUS TOOLS

Utility

Launched multiple campus utility platforms and resource repositories, built by student teams, connecting thousands of active students with study guides and exam papers.

2023

INSOMNIA HACKATHON SCALE

Growth

Insomnia expanded to a national-scale hackathon, attracting over 1,500 builders and industry sponsors from top technical firms across India.

2026

PRESENT DAY

Active

Active across 8 technical domains with 120+ active builders, 45+ deployed projects, and a legacy of uninterrupted technical community building.
